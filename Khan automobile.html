About Khan Automobile

A Workshop Built on Experience, Not Excuses

For over two and a half decades, Khan Automobile has been the go-to workshop for car owners across Dharam Colony and greater Gurugram. What started as a small repair setup is today a full-service garage trusted by thousands of families, taxi operators and corporate fleets.

Under the guidance of Mehandi Hasan, our team diagnoses the real problem before touching a single bolt. No unnecessary part replacement. No inflated bills. Only genuine parts and workmanship that lasts.

  • Genuine spare parts only — sourced from authorised dealers with bills.
  • Transparent estimates shared before any work begins.
  • All brands serviced — Maruti, Hyundai, Honda, Toyota, Tata, Mahindra & more.
  • Open all 7 days — because car trouble doesn't wait for Monday.
What We Do

Complete Car Care Services

Engine Repair

AC Service & Repair

Brake & Suspension

Car Electricals

Denting & Painting

Wheel Alignment
